Classify every `ItemKind` for `item_exists()`: Oracle used to answer `SCHEMA`, `TYPE` and `SEQUENCE` and return `False` for every other kind — including `TABLE`, so a caller asking whether a table existed was told no. `TABLE`, `INDEX`, `TABLESPACE` and `FUNCTION` now answer from their catalogues, and `DOMAIN` joins them on 23ai and newer; kinds the engine does not have at all (extension, partition function, partition scheme, full-text catalogue) answer `False` without issuing SQL, an undeclared kind is refused by name, and the version-gated `DOMAIN` refuses when the server version was never measured. The declared sets partition the enum, and both introspectors answer alike. An unpartitioned table's partition state also answers `PartitionState(scheme=None)` rather than `None` at every entry point — `parse_partition_state()`, `get_partition_state()` and the `partitioning` slot of `get_table_state()`, sync and async — so core's neutral record is the single answer shape for "not partitioned".
