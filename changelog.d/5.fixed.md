Stop the Oracle backend from discarding or over-claiming declared DDL: `supports_temporal_tables` is declared `False` instead of left unstated, unrenderable `ALTER SCHEMA` capabilities are no longer advertised, `supports_comment` is renamed to `supports_comment_on` and declared comments are no longer dropped, `CREATE VIEW ... WITH CHECK OPTION` is gated on `supports_view_check_option`, `DROP TYPE` no longer applies a default `VALIDATE`, and `DROP TABLE ... PURGE` renders independently of `CASCADE`.
