Remove `OracleIdentifierMixin` (`mixins/identifier.py`, exported from `mixins` until now) and the auto-generated `OracleShowDialectSupport` protocol (`protocols/dialect_capabilities.py`): `format_identifier` is now defined once on `OracleDialect`, and qualified names are rendered by `OracleNamespaceMixin` (`mixins/namespace.py`). `supports_auto_increment` is gone, superseded by `supports_auto_increment_column`, which answers `False` because `AUTO_INCREMENT` is a different, parameterless mechanism Oracle does not spell. Extensions that imported the removed modules should import `format_identifier` from the dialect and naming from `mixins.namespace`.
